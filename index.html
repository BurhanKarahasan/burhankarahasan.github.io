Burhan Karahasan

Building robots small enough to navigate the human body — and the algorithms that guide them.

I'm a graduate researcher at Koç University's Medical Robotics Laboratory, where I work under the supervision of Prof. Metin Sitti on the design, control, and optimization of medical microrobots — miniature robotic systems and the algorithms that let them operate safely inside the body. I hold dual bachelor's degrees in Mechanical and Electrical & Electronics Engineering (Summa Cum Laude, 3.95/4.00) and am completing an MSc in Mechanical Engineering, with the goal of pursuing a PhD focused on making robotic intervention more precise, less invasive, and more accessible to clinicians.

About

My research sits at the intersection of robotic design, real-time control, and AI-driven medical solutions. I'm drawn to problems where a better algorithm directly improves a patient outcome — for example, planning patient-specific surgical paths from MRI/CT imaging to minimize tissue damage in minimally invasive procedures.

I graduated third in my class (2018–2024) and was elected a Presidential Fellow of Koç University in 2024. My studies have been supported by a full merit scholarship, multiple Vehbi Koç Honor Awards, and the DEVAK Scholarship, and I completed a dedicated AI track program that sharpened how I integrate machine learning into robotics. What motivates me most is the practical end of the work: putting reliable, intelligent tools into the hands of the clinicians who use them.

Selected Projects

Robotic-Guided Brain Biopsy Optimization: Developed algorithms to create patient-specific surgical paths using MRI/CT imaging, minimizing tissue damage and enhancing precision in minimally invasive brain surgeries. Utilized Procrustes-based registration for accurate imaging-to-patient alignment, improving surgical outcomes.

Mechanical Ventilation System: Engineered a real-time wireless sensor system for non-invasive ventilatory support, optimizing performance in single-limb passive vented circuits. This work, published in 2023, enhanced efficiency in critical care settings.
